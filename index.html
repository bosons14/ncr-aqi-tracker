Pollutant data: Open-Meteo Air Quality API, which serves the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) model (it assimilates satellite observations, e.g. Sentinel-5P & MODIS AOD). Wind: Open-Meteo forecast. Farm fires: NASA FIRMS VIIRS (via fetch_fires.py). The Indian AQI here is computed from PM2.5/PM10 only using CPCB breakpoints and a trailing 24-h mean of modelled values. It is an estimate, not an official CPCB reading.
